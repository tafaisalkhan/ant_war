# Ant Colony Wars Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

Ant Colony Wars (`com.antcolonywars.game`) saves level progress, stars, coins, colony upgrades, daily reward streaks, sound preferences, and ad-removal status in local app storage on your device. Ant Colony Wars does not run its own account or game data server.

## Advertising

When advertising is enabled in an Android release build, Google AdMob may serve banner, interstitial, and optional user-initiated rewarded ads. AdMob may process device identifiers, IP address, approximate location inferred from IP address, ad views, interactions, and diagnostics under [Google's advertising policies](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads). When ads are unavailable or disabled, no ads are shown.

## Purchases

When Google Play purchases are enabled in a release build, Google Play handles payment and purchase verification for Remove Ads (`remove_ads`), coin packs (`coins_1000`, `coins_2000`, `coins_3000`), and developer support (`coffee`). Ant Colony Wars stores only the resulting game entitlement or coin balance locally and does not receive payment card details.

## In-App Updates

Ant Colony Wars uses the official Google Play In-App Updates API (`com.google.android.play:app-update`) to check for and install newer versions published on Google Play.

## Data Control

You can erase locally saved game data at any time by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it. Google's handling of ad and purchase data follows its own retention and deletion policies.

Ant Colony Wars does not ask players to create an account or enter personal information. Parents and guardians should supervise children's use of ads and purchases.
